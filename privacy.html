Privacy Policy¶

Last updated: February 06, 2026

Introduction¶

ClassDeck (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

By using ClassDeck, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect¶

When you use ClassDeck, we may access the following types of information via your Google Account, based on the permissions (scopes) you grant:

  1. Google User Data:

    1. Basic profile information (name, email address, profile picture) to identify you and personalize your experience.

    2. Google Classroom data (Courses, CourseWork, student submissions) to display your dashboard and assignments.

  2. Usage Data: a. We may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the application’s performance.

How We Use Your Information¶

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Service Provision: To display your Google Classroom courses, assignments, and relevant details within the ClassDeck dashboard.

  • Personalization: To customize the interface with your name and preferences.

  • Functionality: To allow you to organize, filter, and track your assignments locally.

Data Storage and Security¶

  • Access Tokens: Your Google OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored securely in our database. We use industry-standard encryption (AES/Fernet) to protect these tokens at rest.

  • Data Retention: We only retain data necessary to provide the service. You can revoke access at any time via your Google Account settings, which will render stored tokens invalid.

Third-Party Services¶

ClassDeck use use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties.

Your Rights¶

You have the right to: * Revoke ClassDeck’s access to your Google Account via Google Security Settings. * Request deletion of your data from our local records by contacting us.
